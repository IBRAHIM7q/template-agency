Our Web Development Process

Learn how we develop your website from idea to completion.

1. Planning and Requirements Analysis

In this phase, we work with you to define the goals and requirements of your web project. We analyze your target audience, create personas, and define the functionalities your website needs.

Our planning phase includes:

  • Requirements analysis and goal setting
  • Target audience analysis and persona creation
  • Sitemap and information architecture
  • Technical specifications and technology selection

2. Design and Wireframing

Based on the requirements, we create wireframes and design concepts for your website. We value user-friendly design that reflects your brand identity while providing an optimal user experience.

Our design process includes:

  • Wireframing and prototyping
  • UI/UX design
  • Responsive design for all devices
  • Design feedback and iterations

3. Development and Programming

In this phase, we transform the design into functioning code. Our developers work with modern technologies and best practices to create a powerful, secure, and maintainable website.

Our development phase includes:

  • Frontend development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
  • Backend development and database integration
  • CMS integration (if needed)
  • Implementation of functionalities and features

4. Testing and Quality Assurance

Before we publish your website, we conduct extensive testing to ensure everything works flawlessly. We test on various devices and browsers and optimize performance.

Our testing process includes:

  • Functionality tests
  • Cross-browser and cross-device tests
  • Performance optimization
  • Security checks

5. Launch and Support

After successful testing, we publish your website and ensure everything runs smoothly. We also offer post-launch support and maintenance to ensure your website remains current and secure.

Our launch phase includes:

  • Deployment on the production server
  • Domain configuration and SSL setup
  • Content management training
  • Ongoing maintenance and support
